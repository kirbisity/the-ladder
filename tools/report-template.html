Career odds by character and employer

What the runs say

Employer tiers, on average

Every character's careers averaged together, one employer kind at a time. Higher tiers fire more people and pay more; the money arrives sooner.

Each character, each tier

Pick an industry and a measure. Each cell is the share (or average) across the careers played at that kind of employer; "real mix" lets employers vary and job hops cross tiers.

Twelve-hour weeks

The same careers in tech, played at twelve hours a day every quarter, resting only when burnout forces it. This is where the characters' hidden tolerances show.

The unemployment spiral

Married players lose their job and decline every offer, so the search runs as long as it can. Divorce takes half of everything; past the debt cushion, the apartment goes.
